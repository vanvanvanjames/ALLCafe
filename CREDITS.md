## Not photographs

The brand marks in `assets/` — the logo lockups, the mug mark, the favicon, the
speckle pattern and the UI icons — are original SVGs drawn for this project. They
are not stock imagery and carry no third-party licence.

The illustrated SVGs that these photographs replaced are kept for reference in
`archive/generated-svg/`, mirroring their original folder structure. They are not
referenced by the running site.
