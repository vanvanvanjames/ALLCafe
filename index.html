Why ALL

A small kapihan where everyone gets a seat.

We started with four tables, one secondhand espresso machine, and a rule we still keep: nobody gets turned away for ordering the cheapest thing on the board. Students with one americano and five hours of homework, tricycle drivers on a ten-minute break, titas settling in for the long chika — same welcome, same mugs.

ALL means lahat. It is also what is painted across the enamel tasa we serve everything in — the same speckled tin mug that is already sitting in your lola's kitchen.
