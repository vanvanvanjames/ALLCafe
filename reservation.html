Before you book

Do I need a reservation to come in?

Never. Walk-ins are the whole point of a neighborhood kapihan — reservations just guarantee you a table at a specific hour, which helps on weekends and after 5 PM.

Can we bring a cake?

Yes, and no corkage. Tell us in the notes so we can set aside plates, forks and a table with enough room for the candles.

Is there parking?

Two slots out front and street parking along the side road after 9 AM. Most suki arrive by tricycle or jeep — we're a two-minute ride from LRT-1 Monumento.

Rather just call?

Big groups, catering, or same-day changes are fastest by phone.
