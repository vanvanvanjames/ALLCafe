Palette

Contrast audit — WCAG 2.1

Every text/background pair the site actually uses, measured live from the tokens in base.css. AA needs 4.5:1 for body text and 3:1 for large text (≥24 px, or ≥19 px bold) and for UI borders.

ForegroundBackgroundRatioVerdictSample

Mangga is a background-only colour. At 1.6:1 on enamel it can never carry text; it earns its keep behind ink — badges, the cart dot, price tags. Likewise --dahon is for fills and dots, with --dahon-ink as its text-safe twin.

Typography
